# 照片浏览器 v0.4.19 / Photo Browser v0.4.19

- 视频参数在帧率下方新增「码率」，按 kbps / Mbps 自动显示视频轨道的平均码率，保留两位小数；无法获取时显示「—」。
- 码率不包含音频与容器开销。旧视频封面缓存会按需刷新，已有会话元数据会补入码率，无需手动清理缓存。
- 补齐简体中文与英文展示，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel，最低 macOS 14；v0.4.12 及以上版本可通过应用内检查更新升级。

- Adds Bitrate below Frame Rate in Video Details, displaying the video track's average bitrate in kbps or Mbps with two decimal places. Unavailable values appear as “—”.
- Bitrate excludes audio and container overhead. Older video-cover caches refresh on demand, and existing session metadata gains the new field without manually clearing caches.
- Includes English and Simplified Chinese labels. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel on macOS 14 or later; users of v0.4.12 or later can upgrade through Check for Updates.
