# 照片浏览器 v0.4.20 / Photo Browser v0.4.20

- 照片右键菜单增加前置图标，覆盖复制、移到待删除目录、删除、全屏预览、Camera Raw、格式子菜单及 Finder；网格、胶片栏、大图和全屏预览保持一致。
- 视频播放、实况照片播放与返回照片、视频导出菜单也显示对应图标。
- 设置窗口改为紧凑的分页布局，便于切换和查找选项。
- 支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel，最低 macOS 14；v0.4.12 及以上版本可通过应用内检查更新升级。

- Adds leading icons to photo context menus for copying, moving to the deletion folder, deleting, fullscreen preview, Camera Raw, format selection, and Finder across the grid, filmstrip, large preview, and fullscreen preview.
- Adds matching icons for video playback, Live Photo playback and returning to the still photo, and video export.
- Organizes settings into compact tabs for easier navigation.
- Supports Apple Silicon and Intel on macOS 14 or later; users of v0.4.12 or later can upgrade through Check for Updates.
